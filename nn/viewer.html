bro-agent NN — commit triage

Pure-stdlib MLP. Ranks commits by how much their diff stats look like past test-touching commits. Triage only: it never verifies a commit does (or does not) touch the tests. Train with python -m nn.train, score with python -m nn.score --sha <sha>.
